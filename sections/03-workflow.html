One workflow. Every stakeholder. Confirmed at source.

Ambler replaces the back-and-forth with a structured flow where everyone confirms their own credits.

  1. 1

    Label creates

    Your team starts a registration — track details, initial splits, and an invite to the main artist.

    Draft
  2. 2

    Artist confirms

    The artist reviews the details, confirms their roles and splits, adds contributors, and proposes their shares.

    Artist input
  3. 3

    Contributors accept

    Each contributor sees their proposed roles and splits. They confirm or counter — no chasing, no ambiguity.

  4. 4

    Artist does QA

    The artist reviews all responses, verifies that master and publishing splits total 100%, and submits to the label.

    QA review
  5. 5

    Label ships

    Your team does a final review. Three confirmations — release date, external destinations, internal distribution — then ships.

    Ready to ship
  6. 6

    Data delivered

    Validated metadata flows to PROs, CMOs, publishers, and your distributor or owned release agent. Locked. Audit trail. Done.
